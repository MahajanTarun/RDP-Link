Office RDP is a self-hostable remote desktop app for phones, tablets, and desktops.

Host a device:
• Share this screen with a guest over your local network or a rendezvous server.
• Protect the session with a password you choose.
• See a persistent notification while sharing, with a one-tap stop action.

Connect to a device:
• Open a host address or peer ID and control it from the built-in viewer.
• Tap and type remotely after enabling the accessibility helper.
• Works alongside the Office RDP desktop agent for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Privacy:
• No account and no cloud dependency. Use the public rendezvous or run your own.
• Screen content is only sent to guests that know your password.
• The accessibility helper injects taps and text; it does not read your screen.

Office RDP is open source and designed for small teams that want a simple, direct
remote desktop without a subscription.
